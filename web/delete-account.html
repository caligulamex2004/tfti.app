Account & Data Deletion

Instructions for permanently deleting your TFTI account and associated personal data.

At TFTI ("Thanks For The Invite"), we believe you should have complete ownership and control over your personal data. In accordance with Apple App Store Review Guidelines, Google Play Developer Policies, GDPR, and CCPA, you have the right to delete your account and all associated personal information at any time.

Method 1: Delete Directly in the App (Instant)

The fastest and most direct way to delete your account is from within the TFTI mobile or web app:

  1. Open the TFTI app on your device.
  2. Tap your Profile / Account tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to Account Settings.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm your decision in the prompt.

Note: If you have been logged in for an extended period, Firebase may request a quick re-authentication via SMS code to ensure account security before processing deletion.

Method 2: Request Deletion via Email

If you no longer have access to the app or device, you can submit a manual deletion request by contacting our support team:

Send an email from your verified address or contact us at:

tfti.bearmoose@gmail.com

Please include your registered mobile phone number and username. To protect your security, our team may verify account ownership before completing the deletion.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account?

Permanent Data Removal:

  • Your user profile, username, display name, and avatar are permanently purged.
  • Your phone number mapping is removed from our database.
  • You are removed from all squads and hangout circles.
  • Your push notification device tokens are revoked immediately.

This action is irreversible. Once deleted, your account cannot be restored.
