Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Welcome to TFTI ("Thanks For The Invite"). We respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding personal information. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is collected, used, and shared when you use our mobile and web applications (the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to provide low-friction event coordination and squad management:

  • Account Information: When you register, we collect your phone number (for SMS verification), chosen display name, username, and profile photo/avatar.
  • Squads and Hangouts: Information you create or share within the app, such as hangout titles, dates, locations, notes, RSVPs, vibe check votes, and squad memberships.
  • Chat Messages: Messages and images sent within hangout event chats and squad conversations.
  • Device and Usage Information: Push notification tokens (via Firebase Cloud Messaging) to alert you to hangout invitations, updates, and chat messages.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your identity via secure SMS One-Time Passcodes (OTP).
  • To deliver your hangout invitations, chat messages, and RSVP statuses to your chosen friends and squads.
  • To send push notifications regarding hangout updates, deadlines, and reminders.
  • To maintain system integrity, prevent spam or abuse, and secure your account.

3. How Information Is Shared

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data to data brokers or third-party advertisers.

Your information is only shared in the following contexts:

  • With Your Friends & Squads: Your display name, username, profile photo, and event responses are visible to friends and mutual squad members you choose to connect with.
  • With Infrastructure Providers: We use Google Cloud and Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, and Firebase Cloud Messaging) for hosting, database, and notification delivery under strict confidentiality and security standards.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, subpoena, or to protect the safety and rights of our users.

4. Contacts and Privacy Shield

If you optionally choose to import contacts on mobile devices to discover friends, contact details are processed securely on your device to match registered users and are not sold or exploited for marketing.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your personal information for as long as your account remains active. You have the right to request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time:

  • Directly within the app: Go to Profile > Account Settings > Delete Account.
  • Online without the app: Visit our Account Deletion Request Page.

Upon deletion, your account profile, squad memberships, and personal data are permanently purged from active databases.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us via our Support Page.
