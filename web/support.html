Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a hangout?
Tap the "+" button from the home feed or any squad page. Choose a title, time, location, and set an optional RSVP deadline or vibe check poll, then invite your friends or entire squad.

How do RSVP deadlines work?
Once the deadline passes, friends who have not responded are automatically marked as unable to attend. This prevents awkward delays and gives organizers an accurate headcount automatically.

How do I manage my squads?
From the "Squads" tab, you can view existing squads, create new circles (e.g., sports, dinner, gaming), and invite friends via their unique `@username` or phone number.

I am not receiving SMS verification codes. What should I do?
Ensure you entered your full mobile number including the country code, and verify that your device has active cellular reception. Wait 60 seconds before tapping "Resend Code".
