# tfti.app

A Flutter social mobile app designed to broadcast invites to one or more specific friend circles. A "set it and forget it" kind of invite method. 

## Supported Platforms / Devices

- Android 5.0 Lollipop or higher (Phones & Tablets)
- iOS 15.0 or higher (iPhone/iPad)

## Quick Start

### Logging In

- First time users should tap the "Sign Up" link. Then, choose a username. Choose your country code from the dropdown menu and enter your mobile phone number.  Agree to the ToS and Privacy Policy. Then, tap "Sign Up & Get Login Code".
- Existing users choose yor country code from the drop down menu and type in your mobile phone number. Then tap "Get Login Code".
- You should receive a 6-digit One Time Passcode (OTP) via SMS. 
- Enter the OTP on the next screen and tap "Verify & Enter".

### Import Friends Into Your Squad

- You should first build out your "Squad". Tap the Squad icon on the bottom nav bar.
- Add friends either manually or allowing access to your Contacts list.
- Friends who are not yet on the app will get an SMS with a link to download and join the app.

### Create Your Own Squad Circles

- Create distinct Squad Circles for every type of occassion. Start by tapping the add (+) icon.
- Choose a name for your circle. Have fun or be specific! It's up to you to decide how you'll tell circles apart. 
- The more specific Squad Circles you have, the easier it is to invite only friends who are interested in what you're up to.
- Add friends from your Squad to your new circle. Not all your friends have the same interests.
- Keep adding Squad Circles until you're happy.

### Tell Your Friends What You're Up To

- Tap the Home icon on the bottom nav bar to get started.
- Choose whether you want to let your friends know what's going on or send out a feeler and have your Squad vote on some ideas.

#### Plans Made Easy

Choose this option if you know what you want to do and want to let your Squad know to join you.
- Begin by entering the name of your activity or event. You can be as clear as you like or have fun with it.
- Tap the "Where at?" box. Begin by typing the name of a location or place. Then tap the exact location you want.
- You can add this location to your list of favorite places by tapping the star icon at the end. Choose a nickname for this location.
- Enter the date and time you'll be arriving at the event or location. 
- You can choose an RSVP deadline from 4 hours before to 4 hours after. This gives your pals time to respond. Once the deadline hits, any friends who have not responded will be considered as missing out. 
- Enter any special instructions or details that your friends might need to know. 
- Tap "Squad Needs" if you need to a list of items or favors. Your Squad can then call dibs on what they can bring to the party!
- Tap the "Important" button if you need to let your Squad know that they should read the event details before deciding on joining you or not.
- Next, tap the "Select guests" dropdown menu. You may choose to invite your Entire Squad, or one or more Squad Circles. Tap Done when you're done.
- You also have the option of making one of your friends from the Squad Circles you've chosen as a co-captain. Your co-captain can then add their own Squad Circles to join or add to your Squad Needs.
- When you're done, tap "send out the i's"! Now, those buddies all got the i(nvite)!

#### Vibe Check

Choose this option if you want to throw out a feeler and see what the Squad feels like doing.
- Begin by entering the vibe name. 
- Tap the date and time buttons to choose roughly when you would want to meet up. 
- Optionally, you may enter any notes or tidbits you want your Squad to know. 
- You want to have enough time to get ready and prep. Choose between 24 before start to right at the start of the event. Whichever option has the most votes will automatically be converted into a locked in plan. Only the Captain gets tiebreaker if there's a tie. 
- Provide at least 2 options for you and your Squad to vote on.
- Select the Squad Circles who would interested from the dropdown menu.
- When you're ready to "Throw it out there", tap the button.

## FAQ

<details>
<summary><strong>What if I need to change the time or location of an event?</strong></summary>

As captain, you can tap the edit icon on the top right of the event you would like to change. You can then change any aspect of that event to your liking.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>I have a typo in the nickname for one of my favorite spots. How can I change it?</strong></summary>

Easy! Tap the Account icon on the bottom nav bar. Go to <strong>Preferences &gt; Location Settings &gt; Favorite Places</strong> and tap on any of the entries you want to edit. 

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>How can I change the theme of the app?</strong></summary>

Simply go to <strong>Account &gt; Preferences &gt; Appearance</strong> and tap on any of the themes. Keep it for as long as you like or change it as frequently as you like.

</details>
